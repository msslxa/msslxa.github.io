Packages    
Fermat.zip The library contains several Magma functions computing the birational parametrizations of the Fermat cubic hypersurfaces (of even dimension) and their inverses as described in the paper "Rational points on even dimensional Fermat cubics".
See also Fermat cubics on GitHub.
P1xPn.zip Magma library computing the nef cones and the Mori chamber decompositions of blow-ups of ℙ1 × ℙn in general points.
See also Cox rings of blow-ups of multiprojective spaces on GitHub.
Grass_Magma.zip Magma function computing the Grassmannian of k-planes in an n-dimensional projective space.
Dual_Magma.zip Magma function computing the dual of a projective hypersurface.
Tensor_Decomposition.zip Magma library implementing the algorithms for tensor and polynomial decomposition in "Decomposition algorithms for tensors and polynomials", by A. Laface, A. Massarenti and R. Rischter.
Toric_Def.zip Magma package checking whether a toric variety is defective as described in "On secant defectiveness and identifiability of Segre-Veronese varieties", by A. Laface, A. Massarenti and R. Rischter.
Comon-1.0.m2

Macaulay 2 package checking whether a homogeneous polynomial satisfies Comon's conjecture as described in "On Comon's and Strassen's conjectures", by A. Casarotti, A. Massarenti and M. Mella.

MCD_SBLD.zip Magma package checking whether the Mori chamber and the stable base locus decomposition of a Mori dream space coincide as described in "On Mori chamber and stable base locus decompositions", by A. Laface, A. Massarenti and R. Rischter.
Movable_Complete_Forms.zip Maple scripts computing the extremal rays of the movable cones of spaces of complete forms as described in "On the birational geometry of spaces of complete forms I: collineations and quadrics" and "On the birational geometry of spaces of complete forms II: skew-forms".
Identifiability.m2 Macaulay 2 package implementing the identifiability criteria in "Effective identifiability criteria for tensors and polynomials" by A. Massarenti, M. Mella and G. Staglianò.
Movable.mw Maple script computing the extremal rays of the movable cone of a Mori dream space from the generators of its Cox ring, by A. Massarenti and R. Rischter.
