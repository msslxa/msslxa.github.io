THE IDEA

We are a group of researchers in Algebraic Geometry, particularly interested in higher dimensional Birational Geometry.
After several pilgrimages in the world we found ourselves back in Italy at a reasonable distance to allow us to have a network of collaborations.

Our plan is to regularly organize one-day workshops in various venues, with the main purpose of stimulating discussion and confrontation on current research topics, hoping to involve several other researchers in our project.


For more information please contact one of the organizers via e-mail.
